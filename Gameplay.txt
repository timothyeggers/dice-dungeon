The corew gameplay of slay the spire is weighing defensive and offensive actions. Because life is not restored at end of battle.

- Enemy intents are shown, so you can weigh if you should block, or if you can kill an enemy within a certain time
- You can tank, with the intent of doing enough damage to kill, but also comparing against the next available recovery
- Slay the spire presents a lot of information with intention

If you have a static ability, but the dice is randomized, how do you give player back intention? it feels too random.

A game like rummy gives back player intention by only allowing certain dice throws to be scored once.  and you can take a zero, with the risk and intention of getting a better throw for that scored slot.
